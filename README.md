### 🎯 About Me

I'm a GRC Analyst with 5 years of experience helping financial services and retail organizations secure cardholder data and govern risk across cloud environments. I focus on turning complex, evolving compliance requirements into practical, sustainable controls — not just audit checkboxes.

I've managed PCI DSS compliance programs through the full **v3.2.1 → v4.0.1** transition, including direct work with **Merchant Level 1 and Level 2** organizations, and I specialize in bridging traditional compliance frameworks with cloud-native environments where legacy control models don't map cleanly.
